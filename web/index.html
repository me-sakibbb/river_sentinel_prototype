    How it works

    1. Predict March, before the rains

      In March, the AI studies satellite images. It checks how close the river is, whether it is moving closer, and where land was lost before.

    2. Confirm Every 12 days in the monsoon

      Clouds block normal images during the rains, but radar sees through them. A second AI checks where land is turning into river.

    3. Warn When both agree

      People in High alert areas would get an SMS or voice call in Bangla. Local officials would get a map. This test version sends nothing.

    Limits

    This is a test version. Not every High alert place will lose land, so use it to decide where to check first. The AI cannot see protection work such as geobags, so protected places may be marked by mistake.
