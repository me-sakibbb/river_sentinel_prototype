    How it works

    1. Predict March, before the rains

      The AI studies the dry-season satellite picture: how close the channel is, whether it is moving in, and where land was lost before. Each stretch of bank gets a risk score.

    2. Confirm Every 12 days in the monsoon

      Clouds hide the ground all monsoon, but radar sees through them. A second AI model checks where bank is actually turning into river.

    3. Warn When both agree

      People living on a high-alert stretch would get a Bangla SMS or voice call, and local officials a map. In this prototype nothing is sent.

    Limits

    This is a research prototype. Not every High alert erodes: use it to decide where to check first. The model can't see bank protection such as geobags, so protected banks may be over-flagged.
